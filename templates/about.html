About KisanSetu AI

KisanSetu AI is a digital agricultural marketplace designed to connect farmers and buyers on one platform. Our goal is to make product listing, product discovery and order management simpler through technology.

🎯

Our Mission

To simplify agricultural trading by providing farmers and buyers with an accessible digital platform.

🤝

Our Vision

To support stronger connections between agricultural producers and the people who purchase their products.

💡

Our Approach

To use digital tools for product listings, marketplace browsing and organised order management.

One Platform, Two Communities

Farmers can list and manage their products, while buyers can explore available produce and place orders through the marketplace.
